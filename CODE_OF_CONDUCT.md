# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge 🌈

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming and meteorologically inclusive environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in FreshWDL-rs a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, preferred unit system, or preferred weather.

## Our Standards ✨

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, including Fahrenheit
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism, including from the borrow checker
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the weather
- Showing empathy towards other community members and instruments

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, or weather station coordinates, without explicit permission
- Saying "it's just a static page" in a design review
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## 🌡️ Instrument Conduct

FreshWDL-rs extends this Code of Conduct to the **instruments** and **phenomena** represented in the codebase. All contributors are expected to observe the following:

### 📊 Be Kind to the Barometer

The barometer has been working continuously, without complaint, measuring atmospheric pressure every few seconds for years. It does not get a day off. It does not get a crate with a fun name.

- Do not tap the barometer to "see if it moves". It has feelings, and also it is a digital sensor.
- Do not refer to a falling barometer as "failing". It is **descending with purpose**.
- Do not compare the barometer unfavourably to the thermometer, which has three widgets.

### 🧭 Respect Wind Direction's Citizenship

Wind direction was granted first-class citizenship in [ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-wind-direction-as-a-first-class-citizen.md). Treat it accordingly. Do not refer to it as "index 3".

### 🌧️ Do Not Deprecate Rain

We have been through this. See [ADR-0014](docs/adr/0014-revert-adr-0011-rain-restored.md).

### 🌙 Leave the Moon Alone

The moon does not have unit tests. This is not because it is unimportant. It is because it is too far away. Please do not open PRs asking for moon tests.

### 💧 Humidity

Humidity's request for first-class citizenship (ADR-0019) is under review. Please be patient and supportive during this time.

### 🦀 Ferris

Ferris is a friend. Do not remove Ferris from the console.

## Our Responsibilities 👮

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behaviour, including towards the barometer.

## Enforcement ⚖️

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

Instrument-related complaints will be forwarded to the instrument in question.
